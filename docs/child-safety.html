Child Safety Standards (CSAE)

App: CILMI FOUNDATION (com.cilmifoundation.app)

Last updated: 6 August 2026

These Child Safety Standards describe how CILMI FOUNDATION prevents, detects, reports, and responds to child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

1. Purpose of the app

CILMI FOUNDATION is a private family lineage and mutual-aid app for members of the Cilmi family community. It is not a dating app, public social network, or open chat platform for strangers. Children’s names may appear in the family tree as lineage records managed by adults.

2. Zero-tolerance policy

We prohibit:

  • Any child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Sexual exploitation, grooming, or solicitation involving minors
  • Uploading, sharing, requesting, or storing sexual content involving minors
  • Using the app to facilitate harm to children

Violations may result in immediate account restriction, profile unlink, content removal, and reporting to relevant authorities where required by law.

3. Prevention standards

  • Access is limited to invited/approved family members through account sign-in and profile linking.
  • New accounts must claim an existing family-tree profile and await admin approval before full access.
  • Roles limit sensitive administrative actions to trusted managers and super admins.
  • The app is not designed for anonymous public posting, open messaging to strangers, or discovery of unknown users.
  • Media uploads (such as profile photos or payment receipts) are limited in purpose and reviewed by admins when used for verification workflows.

4. Detection and response

  • Users and admins may report child-safety concerns by contacting our child-safety point of contact (below).
  • Reports are reviewed by designated administrators as quickly as practical.
  • We remove or restrict violating content/accounts when identified.
  • We preserve relevant records as needed for investigation and lawful reporting.

5. Reporting to authorities

Where legally required, CILMI FOUNDATION will report confirmed or credible CSAM / child exploitation concerns to the appropriate regional or national authorities and cooperate with lawful investigations.

6. How to report a child safety concern

  1. Email info@abdirahmaan.dev with the subject: CILMI FOUNDATION child safety report.
  2. Include what you saw, when it happened, the member/profile name if known, and any screenshots you can safely provide.
  3. Do not redistribute suspected CSAM. Send a report to us and to local authorities if someone is in immediate danger.

7. Designated point of contact

Child safety / CSAE compliance contact for CILMI FOUNDATION:
Email: info@abdirahmaan.dev

This contact is able to discuss the app’s CSAM prevention practices and compliance with applicable child-safety requirements.

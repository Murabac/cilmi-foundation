Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026

Also see: Request data deletion

CILMI FOUNDATION (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) is a family lineage and mutual-aid mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

The App is operated for the CILMI FOUNDATION family community. Package name: com.cilmifoundation.app.

Contact for privacy questions: arahman.murabac@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect:

  • Account information: mobile phone number and password (used to create and sign in to your account).
  • Profile information: full name, family-tree links (parent/spouse relationships), demographic details, marital status, occupation, city, care rating, and optional profile photo.
  • Contribution / payment information: monthly contribution amounts, payment status, transaction references, and optional payment receipts or self-reported payment notices for admin verification.
  • Treasury / aid records: donations to the family pool and aid disbursements recorded by authorized staff.
  • Technical data: basic device and app usage needed to run the service (for example, network requests to our backend). We do not sell advertising profiles.

3. How we use information

We use the information to:

  • Create and manage your account and family-tree profile
  • Operate monthly contributions, payment verification, and pool balance
  • Allow authorized admins/treasury staff to manage members and finances
  • Enable call or WhatsApp contact with a member when a phone number is saved
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and improve the App

4. How we store and process data

App data is stored and processed using Supabase (cloud database and authentication). Data is transmitted over HTTPS.

Access inside the community is role-based (for example family member, manager, treasury, and super admin). Sensitive administrative actions are limited to authorized roles.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared only:

  • Within the family organization as needed for the App’s features (for example, admins reviewing payments)
  • With service providers that host our backend (Supabase), under their terms and security controls
  • When required by law or to protect the rights and safety of the community

If you tap Call or WhatsApp for a member’s number, your device opens that external app. Those services are governed by their own privacy policies.

6. Permissions the App may use

  • Internet: required to sign in and sync data
  • Phone / dialer: optional, to place calls to saved member numbers or run payment USSD flows
  • Photos / media: optional, if you upload a profile photo or payment receipt

7. Data retention

We keep account and family records while the member remains part of the community database. Authorized administrators may update or remove member records according to internal policy. You may request correction or deletion of your account-linked data by contacting us at the email above.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for family administration and adult members managing household/family records. Children’s names may appear in the family tree as part of lineage data. The App is not directed at children for independent use, and we do not knowingly collect data from children for marketing.

9. Security

We use authentication, role-based access, and encrypted transport (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please use a strong password and keep your phone secure.

10. Your choices

  • You may update profile details available in the App
  • You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data
  • You may stop using the App and request account unlink/removal where applicable

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after updates means you accept the revised policy.

Request deletion of your data

App: CILMI FOUNDATION (com.cilmifoundation.app)

Last updated: 6 August 2026

This page explains how users of the CILMI FOUNDATION Android app can request that their personal data be deleted.

How to request deletion

  1. Email arahman.murabac@gmail.com from the phone number or email associated with your account when possible.
  2. Use the subject line: CILMI FOUNDATION data deletion request.
  3. Include:
    • Your full name as shown in the app
    • The mobile number used to sign in (with country code)
    • A short confirmation that you want your account data deleted
  4. We will verify ownership of the account and process the request.

What we delete

After verification, we delete or unlink account-related personal data, including where applicable:

  • Sign-in / authentication link to your account
  • Phone number stored on your profile for login/contact
  • Profile photo / avatar you uploaded
  • Optional personal fields such as occupation and city
  • Payment receipts or self-submitted payment proofs tied to your account
  • Pending profile-claim requests for your account

What may be kept

Some family-organization records may be retained when they are required for community administration, financial accountability, or legal/accounting needs:

  • Family-tree lineage entries (name and parent links) that form part of the shared family registry may remain as historical family records, or be anonymized/unlinked from login where deletion of the tree entry would break the community registry
  • Aggregated contribution / treasury ledger amounts needed for pool accounting may be retained without your login access
  • Records we must keep under applicable law, dispute resolution, or fraud prevention

Timeline

We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days. If more time is needed (for example to confirm identity), we will reply by email.
